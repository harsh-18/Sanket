    Why we don't claim "99% Accuracy"

    Speech accuracy varies significantly by accent, microphone quality, ambient noise, code-mixing and spontaneous speech. Independent scientific benchmarks place Hindi ASR around ~16% Word Error Rate (~84% accuracy) on clean read speech. Sanket prioritizes honesty: confident predictions are confirmed, uncertain predictions ask for clarification.

    Architecture & Roadmap

    How Sanket Works

    Three decoupled layers. One shared conversation. Sender and receiver choose modalities independently.

    A · Input Layer

    How users express themselves:

    • Speech (hi-IN, en-IN)
    • ISL Gloss cards (validated)
    • Experimental Handshape camera
    • Assistive text / Switch input

    B · AI Layer

    How inputs are processed:

    • Speech recognition + confidence
    • Language identification
    • Contextual translation
    • Uncertainty → confirmation

    C · Output Layer

    How recipients receive:

    • Neural Spoken audio (TTS)
    • Screen-reader compatible text
    • Validated ISL video playback
    • Haptic sensory alerts
