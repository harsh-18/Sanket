    04 · Architecture

    How Sanket Works — 3 Decoupled Layers

    Each participant independently chooses their own transmission and reception modalities. AI handles multimodal orchestration.

    Layer 01

    Input Modality Capture

    Captures spoken Hindi/English via Web Speech, 12 validated ISL glosses, or keyboard/assistive switches.

    Layer 02

    AI & Confidence Gate

    Processes recognition, language identification, phrasebook translation, and uncertainty verification.

    Layer 03

    Adaptive Output Delivery

    Synthesizes spoken audio (TTS), displays high-contrast screen-reader text, or triggers ISL video clips.

    Real-World Communication Scenarios

    Built for Universal Everyday Access

    Hearing ↔ Deaf

    Doctor & Patient Consultations

    Doctor speaks medical diagnosis in Hindi; Deaf patient receives large text and validated ISL gloss. Patient responds via ISL gloss.

    Deaf ↔ Blind

    Direct Accessible Conversation

    Deaf user signs ISL glosses; Blind user receives immediate spoken audio via TTS and speaks back in voice without an intermediary.

    Future Roadmap

    Delivery Call Integration (Zomato / Swiggy)

    When a delivery driver calls, Sanket intercepts call audio, transcribes to text/ISL in real time, and translates the reply back to speech.

    Why we do not claim "99% Accuracy"

    Speech accuracy is never a single fixed number. It varies with accent, microphone distance, background noise, and spontaneous conversational speech. Scientific benchmarks place Hindi ASR at roughly ~16% Word Error Rate (~84% accuracy) under clean read conditions. Sanket prioritizes honesty: confident predictions are confirmed, uncertain predictions trigger a quick clarify step.
